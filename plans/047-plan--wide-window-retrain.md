# Plan 047 — is data volume the bottleneck? Train on the whole plateau corpus

**Status:** Running (launched 2026-09-29, `scripts/exp047_wide_window.sh`, out `runs/exp047/`).

## Why

`runs/loopmix16x9` was stopped at gen22 (2026-09-29) on a plateau: against the frozen gen13 anchor,
gen14-21 read 0.50-0.545 on 14x7 and 0.445-0.535 on 16x9, rolling-3 after gen20 0.503 / 0.492
(n=300, ±0.025). Validation loss kept falling the whole time (0.755 gen14 → 0.718 gen21) and the
restore step kept landing early (2500-32500), which is plan 040's mechanism 1: each generation's
fine-tune extracts what little is new in its window, then has nothing left.

Plan 040's conclusion was that the corpus has to change: more *new* drives per generation. This is
the cheapest test of that. It uses no new games.

## Why this is not a re-run of plan 040

Plan 040 pooled gen02-08, which came from nets of **rising** strength, so most of the wide pool was
data from weaker nets. It tested "does a wider window help", not "does more data from the current
net help". Here the curve is flat from gen13 to gen21: the eleven generations gen12-22 were
generated by nets within noise of each other. Pooling them approximates 11 × 4800 games from the
current champion, which is what a larger `GAMES_PER_SHARD` would buy.

gen11 and earlier are excluded: they were generated before `1458608` (second-half rerolls) and
carry wrong reroll labels.

## Arms

Same pool for both: train = shards 0-3,5,6 of gen12-22; val = gen22 shards 4,7 (in no training pool,
and never seen by gen21). Loop settings otherwise (cq tau 100, value blend 0.5, value weight 0.25
per drive, 3 epochs, select on combined).

| arm | init | lr |
|---|---|---|
| `wide_warm` | gen21 (champion) | 2e-4 |
| `wide_scratch` | random | 1e-3 |

**Control:** gen21, benchmarked by the loop against the same anchor, same `--seed 0`:
0.545 (14x7), 0.480 (16x9) — 0.513 pooled over 200 games.

**Eval:** 100 games vs `anchor_mix16x9_gen13.onnx` on each of 14x7 and 16x9, 500 iterations,
`BLOOD_MCTS_BUDGET=visits` — the loop's benchmark minus 12x9. 200 games per arm, SE ≈ 0.032.

## Decision (pre-committed, on the pooled 200 games; not on val loss)

- **Either arm ≥ 0.56:** data volume is the bottleneck. Relaunch the loop with more games per
  generation and a wider window (and, if scratch is the winner, a periodic from-scratch retrain).
- **Both ≤ 0.53:** data volume is not the lever on this corpus. Skip the 1000-game relaunch; go to
  self-play exploration (plan 032 #4) or capability work.
- **In between:** rerun the better arm at 200 more games before deciding.
